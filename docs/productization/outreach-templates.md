# Outreach templates

Rule from the GTM decision: never ask for a generic demo. Offer a real brief
about something the prospect already tracks, then send the actual output.
Fill every `[bracket]` before sending — do not send these verbatim.

## LinkedIn connection note / opening DM (short)

> Hi [Name] — I built a research system that ranks competitive intel by
> source credibility first (not relevance or recency), suppresses recycled
> coverage instead of padding the result count, and always shows the
> strongest case against its own conclusion. Want me to run it on
> [competitor / pricing move / market shift] you track and send you the
> actual brief? No charge, no deck.

## Email / InMail (longer)

**Subject:** A free brief on [Competitor Name] — no pitch, just the output

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm not going to ask for 30 minutes to watch a demo. Instead: give me one
> competitor, pricing move, or market shift you're already tracking, and I'll
> run it through a research system I built and send you the real output —
> free, no strings.
>
> What makes it different from "AI + citations" tools: every result is ranked
> by source class *before* relevance — a rule the system is not allowed to
> break, even automatically. Duplicate and recycled coverage gets suppressed
> rather than padding the count. Every brief carries a mandatory "what
> contradicts this" section; if nothing does, it says so instead of hiding
> the gap.
>
> It's early — I'm working with a small number of design partners to sharpen
> it before a wider release, which is also why this costs you nothing right
> now.
>
> Reply with a company or topic and you'll have a brief back within
> [2-3 business days].
>
> [Your name]
> [JBAnalytics LLC / your title]

## Follow-up after delivering the sample brief

> Here's the brief on [Company/Topic]. A few things worth pointing at:
> - [pick 1-2 concrete lines from the actual output — a sourced claim, the
>   contradiction section, or a novel domain it found]
>
> If this is useful, I'd like to run it as a recurring brief for
> [topic/competitor set] for a few weeks at no cost, in exchange for feedback
> on what's actually useful vs. noise. Open to that?

## PMA Slack / SCIP community posts (not cold outreach — participate first)

Do not lead with a pitch in these communities. Post the same honest thing you
would to a peer: a real finding from a brief, framed as "here's something my
research tool surfaced this week that I hadn't seen elsewhere" — with the
method (source-class-first ranking) mentioned in passing, not sold. Let people
ask what it is.

## What never to say

- "Trusted by [N] companies" — there are none yet.
- Any specific accuracy/ROI percentage not measured on this system, this
  data. (The "4.35% ROI" / "80% accuracy" figures the system found for a
  *tennis* mission are from papers it cited — never repurpose those as if
  they were this product's own performance numbers.)
- "AI-powered" as the headline — it's the least differentiated word available
  to this category right now.
